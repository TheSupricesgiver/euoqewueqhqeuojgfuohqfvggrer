1. FLOT, Work ($W$), $\Delta U$ & Heat ($Q$) Pg 1–4
1st Law (LOCE) $Q = \Delta U + W \;\;|\;\; dQ = dU + dW$
Work (Path Fn) $dW = P\,dV \implies W = \int_{V_i}^{V_f} P\,dV = \text{Area}(P\text{-}V)$
Sign of $W$ $\text{Exp }(\Delta V>0): +W \;\;|\;\; \text{Comp }(\Delta V<0): -W$
Cyclic ($\Delta U=0$) $Q = W = \text{Loop Area } (\text{CW: } +A, \text{ ACW: } -A)$
Free Expansion $\text{Vacuum } (P_{\text{ext}}=0) \implies W = 0$
Internal Energy $U = \frac{f}{2}nRT = \frac{f}{2}PV \quad (\text{State Fn})$
$\Delta U$ (All Proc.) $\Delta U = nC_V\Delta T = \frac{f}{2}nR\Delta T = \frac{f}{2}\Delta(PV)$
Sign of $\Delta U$ $\Delta T>0 \implies +\Delta U \;\;|\;\; \Delta T<0 \implies -\Delta U$
Molar Heat ($C$) $Q = nC\Delta T \implies C = \frac{Q}{n\Delta T} \in (-\infty, \infty)$
State: $P,V,T,U,S$ Path: $Q,W,C$ Intensive: $P,T,\rho,s,C$ Extensive: $m,V,U,n,S$
2. $C_V, C_P$, Mayer's Relation & Atomicity Pg 7, 9, 10
Const Vol ($C_V$) $C_V = \left(\frac{Q}{n\Delta T}\right)_V = \frac{f}{2}R = \frac{R}{\gamma - 1}$
Const Press ($C_P$) $C_P = \left(\frac{Q}{n\Delta T}\right)_P = \left(\frac{f}{2} + 1\right)R = \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1}$
Mayer's & $\gamma$ $C_P - C_V = R \quad\Big|\quad \gamma = \frac{C_P}{C_V} = 1 + \frac{2}{f}$
Gas Type $f$ $C_V$ $C_P$ $\gamma = 1+\frac{2}{f}$
Monoatomic 3 $\frac{3}{2}R$ $\frac{5}{2}R$ $\frac{5}{3} \approx 1.67$
Diatomic / Lin. 5 $\frac{5}{2}R$ $\frac{7}{2}R$ $\frac{7}{5} = 1.4$

4. Polytropic ($PV^x = c$) & Exponent ($x$) Pg 12

8. Heat Engine, Carnot & Refrigerator / HP Pg 17–20
Heat Engine ($\eta$) $W = Q_H - Q_L \;\;|\;\; \eta = \frac{W}{Q_H} = \frac{Q_H - Q_L}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{Q_L}{Q_H}$
Carnot Cycle $\frac{V_B}{V_A} = \frac{V_C}{V_D} \implies \frac{Q_H}{Q_L} = \frac{T_H}{T_L} \quad (\text{2 Iso + 2 Adia})$
Carnot $\eta_{\max}$ $\eta_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - \frac{T_L}{T_H} \quad (0 \le \eta < 1, \;\; \eta_{\text{any}} \le \eta_{\text{Carnot}})$
Refrigerator $(\text{COP})_{\text{Ref}} = \frac{Q_L}{W} = \frac{Q_L}{Q_H - Q_L} = \frac{T_L}{T_H - T_L} \;\; (>0)$
Heat Pump $(\text{COP})_{\text{HP}} = \frac{Q_H}{W} = \frac{Q_H}{Q_H - Q_L} = \frac{T_H}{T_H - T_L} \;\; (>1)$
COP Relation $(\text{COP})_{\text{HP}} = 1 + (\text{COP})_{\text{Ref}}$
